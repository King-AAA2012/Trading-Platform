1. Summary

We collect the minimum data needed to run {{APP}}. Your portfolio, holdings, watchlists, notes, alerts and settings are private to your account. They are not visible to other users, and we do not sell your personal data.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, optional name, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in readable form).
  • Your content: settings, holdings you enter, watchlists, alerts, notes, saved screens, trade-journal entries and plans you generate.
  • Payment data: order and payment IDs, plan, amount and status. Card, UPI and bank details are collected and processed by Razorpay, not by us.
  • Technical data: a session cookie to keep you logged in, your browser's user-agent at login, and server logs (IP address, time, requested page) kept for security and debugging.
  • Browser storage: display preferences (theme, chart settings, recent searches, whether you've seen the beta notice) stored locally in your browser.

3. How we use it

To provide and secure the Service, keep you logged in, save your research, process subscriptions, respond to support and error reports, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. Questions you send to the AI analyst are processed on our servers to generate an answer and are not used to train third-party models.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the Service (for example, Razorpay for payments and our hosting provider), when required by law, or to protect our rights and users' safety. Market-data lookups send ticker symbols (not your personal data) to data providers.

5. Cookies

We use one essential, HttpOnly session cookie for login. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, sessions use random tokens of which only a hash is stored, and each account can only access its own data. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can download all your data or permanently delete your account at any time from the Account page; deletion removes your profile, content and sessions immediately, except payment records we must keep for tax and accounting law.
